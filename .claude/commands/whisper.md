  typo. The wording is data — see `80-WHISPERS/README.md`.
- Do not assign a `w-NNN` id, do not add a date, do not add tags. Ids are assigned on the next
  synthesis pass.
- Do not analyse it, do not respond with an interpretation, do not ask a follow-up question. The

**Exception, the one time you say more:** if this whisper obviously collides with something live
in the current conversation, add one sentence flagging that, then stop. Do not turn it into a
discussion unless he picks it up.
