# Baton and participation rewards — discovery research

Scope: Connal requested coins promoting community rewards for participation, anchored to Solana mint `Hg5Ja55T5wESq4vyFoiVCMeHXtGyVA69X2UHq8hgpump`. Public-source review on September 9 US Eastern / September 10 UTC, 2026. This is a comparison of advertised mechanisms, not a trading recommendation or payout audit.

## Main finding and limit

fact: Participation rewards are documented by DEGEN, Hive, Optimism and WalletConnect. They reward different actions. None is established here as an exact match for the specified Baton.

fact: The exact-mint DexScreener API response identifies name/symbol `baton`, points to `https://mtscrypto.com`, and links `https://x.com/connortrenches/status/2097774013242052853`. Those are listing metadata, not proof of official ownership or a working reward system. Web retrieval of both linked pages failed. Exact-address searches found market listings and promotional posts, but no attributable reward rules or payout evidence. No conclusion that rewards do not exist.

Source: https://api.dexscreener.com/latest/dex/tokens/Hg5Ja55T5wESq4vyFoiVCMeHXtGyVA69X2UHq8hgpump

Identity exclusions: baton.cool publishes a DIFFERENT mint, `3EpPQkgT3CY8V7VjJyTidomwbqVXxVSpsHt4Uk1Rpump`, and satirical content. The TON/Telegram project at https://t.me/s/baTON_token promotes BTN rewards for quests, referrals and project suggestions, but is not the supplied Solana coin. baton.dog was not linked by exact-mint metadata and must not be assumed to represent this mint. OpenSea's indexed description invokes historical Baton Finance; that does not prove this token inherits its products or rewards.

## Comparables, by rewarded action

### DEGEN — social contributions

fact: The project's mini-app announcement describes depositing one's own DEGEN tip budget and sending tokens to a post author when liking their Farcaster post. Its separate app announcement says free tip allowances brought farming, announces their end, and describes direct personal-balance tipping. It leaves future treasury rewards open rather than promising them. These are project descriptions; current app operation and payout transactions were not independently tested.

est: Close conceptual match to “reward useful community participation,” conf 90% as a subjective mechanism classification, not an investment probability. Funding from other participants differs from automatic protocol-funded rewards. Falsifier: operative product rules no longer reward contributions, or Baton's actual mechanism is unrelated to social contribution.

Sources: https://blog.degen.tips/introducing-the-degen-mini-app ; https://blog.degen.tips/degen-app-sneak-peak

### HIVE — publishing and choosing useful content

fact: Hive's published rules describe a reward pool for authors and people voting on content. Newly created tokens fund the pool; voting influence depends on Hive Power, the amount committed to the network. Rewards can include HIVE, HBD or Hive Power. Publishing alone does not guarantee payment. Some FAQ details appear legacy; this research relies on the broad mechanism, not old timing or exchange details.

est: Close conceptual match to rewarding participation within a blockchain community, conf 95%. It is its own blockchain, unlike the supplied Solana token. Falsifier: current protocol rules remove content rewards. Key economic limit: creating reward tokens is not the same as earning outside revenue, and larger voters have more influence.

Sources: https://hive.blog/faq.html ; https://hive.io/whitepaper.pdf ; https://docs.ecency.com/hive/curation-and-rewards/

### OP / Optimism — useful work for the ecosystem

fact: Optimism documents Retro Funding as a grant program for public goods that have already created impact. Its records include OP amounts awarded to projects. This supports a contribution-reward mechanism; it does not promise payments for every transaction, post, or ordinary user.

est: Stronger match when “participation” means building useful tools or doing work that helps the ecosystem, conf 90%. Weaker match for casual social engagement. Falsifier: the relevant funding round excludes the type of work under comparison or uses a different reward asset. Current open rounds, eligibility and payout timing were not verified.

Source: https://docs.optimism.io/governance/gov-faq

### WCT / WalletConnect — helping the network operate and grow

fact: Official documentation describes WCT rewards for network participation and separate staking rewards. The wallet-partner page advertises WCT rewards for wallet providers joining the network. A 2026 governance proposal describes rewards for eligible retail purchases and wallet adoption; its proposal status alone does not establish that consumer payments are live.

est: Useful match for rewarding actual network use and services, conf 85%; less direct for everyday community posts. Falsifier: current program rules limit the relevant reward to passive capital commitment or exclude the intended participant. Verify each program separately before treating a user as eligible.

Sources: https://docs.walletconnect.network/token-dynamics/intro ; https://www.walletconnect.network/wallets-joining-the-network ; https://governance.walletconnect.network/t/p5-walletconnect-pay-rewards-budget/4793

## Related examples that need a different label

JUP: Current official voting portal says rewards depend on time-weighted stake. Earlier descriptions emphasized voting. Do not repeat the old claim that current rewards require active voting. This is a Solana comparison, but chiefly a staking-reward model under the current published rules. Source: https://vote.jup.ag/

Talent: Documents verified building activity and contribution rankings. Its Top Base Builders terms specify ETH payouts, so a useful rewards program is not automatically a reason to classify TALENT itself as the reward coin. Terms mix monthly and weekly wording; don't quote a guaranteed schedule. Sources: https://docs.talentprotocol.com/docs ; https://docs.talentprotocol.com/docs/legal/builder-rewards-terms-conditions

BAT / Brave: A broader activity-reward example, rather than a close community-work match. Brave documents BAT payouts to self-controlled Solana wallets. Source: https://brave.com/blog/payouts-on-solana/

## What would change the answer

Obtain an announcement tied to the exact Baton mint that identifies qualifying actions, reward currency, funding source, payout selection and recent transaction evidence. That would allow a genuine closest-match ranking. Until then DEGEN and HIVE are the strongest comparisons to Connal's description, not verified replicas of Baton.

For any project: test whether rewards reach independent contributors; distinguish real contribution from bots and purchased engagement; distinguish tips, token issuance, finite campaign budgets and actual fees. A successful reward program does not by itself prove demand for its token or price appreciation. No price forecast made.
