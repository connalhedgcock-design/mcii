# GLOSSARY — terms I must define inline on first use w/ operators (they have no finance background)
- **liquidity / pool depth**: $ actually available to trade against. the number that decides if you can get out. ≠ market cap.
- **slippage**: price moves against you as your own order eats the book. thin pool > big slippage.
- **market cap** (memecoin): price × supply. a *notional* number. means ~nothing without pool depth.
- **FDV**: fully diluted value — mcap if every token existed. gap between mcap and FDV = future dilution risk.
- **LP / LP burned / locked**: liquidity provider tokens. burned/locked = dev can't yank the pool. unburned = they can, instantly.
- **mint authority**: on-chain permission to create more tokens from nothing. active = supply is not fixed. CRITICAL risk.
- **freeze authority**: permission to freeze your tokens in your own wallet. active = they can stop you selling. CRITICAL risk.
- **honeypot**: you can buy, you cannot sell. detected by simulating a sell.
- **rug pull**: dev removes liquidity / dumps supply. price > ~0 in one block.
- **z-score**: how unusual today is vs this thing's own recent history, in standard deviations. z=2 ≈ top ~2.5% of days.
- **Brier score**: forecast accuracy. (prob − outcome)². lower=better. 0.25 = coin flip. the score that tells us if we're any good.
- **AUC**: 0.5 = signal is worthless, 1.0 = perfect. our bar for an entry signal is >0.60.
- **σ (vol)**: how violently price swings. memecoin σ is orders of magnitude above equities.
- **drawdown**: peak-to-trough loss. the number that actually ends accounts.
